## Introduction

Gurgle Sheets is a parody of Google Sheets designed to entertain while providing a familiar spreadsheet experience. It includes various features that mimic the functionality of Google Sheets, but with a humorous twist. This documentation aims to guide you through its features, installation, and usage.

## Features

Gurgle Sheets offers a range of features that make it both fun and functional:

- **Spreadsheet Functionality**: Create, edit, and manage spreadsheets with ease.
- **Custom Formulas**: Use unique formulas that add a comedic touch.
- **Templates**: Access a variety of pre-made templates for different scenarios.
- **Collaboration Tools**: Share your sheets with friends for collaborative fun.
- **Export Options**: Download your sheets in various formats, including CSV and PDF.

## Usage

After installation, you can begin using Gurgle Sheets. Here’s how:

1. **Create a New Sheet**: Click on "New" to start a blank spreadsheet.
2. **Input Data**: Enter your data in the cells. Use custom formulas to add humor.
3. **Save Your Work**: Regularly save your sheets to avoid losing your progress.
4. **Share with Friends**: Use the share feature to invite others to collaborate.

### Custom Formulas

Gurgle Sheets includes a range of custom formulas. Here are a few examples:

- `=LOL(A1)` - Returns a joke based on the value in cell A1.
- `=GURGLE(A1, B1)` - Combines the contents of A1 and B1 with a funny twist.
